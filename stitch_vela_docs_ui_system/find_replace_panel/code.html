Executive Summary: Q3 Performance

The third quarter of this fiscal year has demonstrated remarkable resilience in our core markets, despite ongoing macroeconomic headwinds. Our strategic pivot towards cloud-native solutions has yielded a 24% year-over-year growth in ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue), significantly outpacing our initial projections outlined in the Q1 strategic offsite.

Customer acquisition costs (CAC) have stabilized, and we are beginning to see the anticipated efficiencies from our revamped digital marketing funnel. However, retention metrics in the SMB segment remain a focal point for improvement as we head into Q4. The product team's roadmap prioritizing ease-of-use and accelerated onboarding flows is critical to addressing this specific challenge.

Furthermore, operational expenses (OpEx) were managed tightly, resulting in a healthier EBITDA margin compared to the previous two quarters. This financial discipline provides us with the necessary capital flexibility to explore targeted M&A opportunities that align with our long-term vision of becoming the premier holistic platform for enterprise productivity.
